Chapter 01 — The Beginning

Every story
starts somewhere.

Mine started on an ordinary winter day. No big moment, no dramatic scene — just a new person arriving in the world, surrounded by family and the kind of small, quiet memories that slowly turn into who you are.

I think that's how most stories actually begin. Not with a spotlight, but with normal days that you don't realise matter until much later.

Chapter 02 — Who I Am

Mostly, I like
the quiet things.

I'm not the loudest person in the room, and I don't really need to be. I like peaceful moments — slow mornings, calm evenings, and the kind of silence where you can finally hear yourself think.

Sleep is genuinely one of my favourite things. A good nap can fix more than people admit. I like simple things more than complicated ones: cheese pizza, time to relax, and days that don't ask too much of me.

I try to be kind. I don't always get it right — but I think it matters more than being impressive. I'm still discovering what I'm good at, and I've made peace with the fact that it's going to take a little time.

“You don't have to have everything figured out to be a work in progress.”

Chapter 03 — Things I Enjoy

A few things I
actually like doing.

These aren't achievements or a list of talents. They're just the small pieces that make up an ordinary week for me.

01 — Judo

Learning to stay calm

I practice judo. It taught me patience, and that falling is just part of learning how to stand properly.

02 — Drawing

Starting from nothing

I draw a little. I'm still getting better at it, but I like the feeling of a blank page slowly becoming something.

03 — Coding

Trying, failing, retrying

I'm learning to code. A lot of it is trial and error — but fixing something after struggling with it feels genuinely good.

04 — Websites

Ideas you can see

I like building websites — turning an idea in my head into something you can actually open, scroll through and use.

None of these things make up my whole personality. They're just pieces of it — things I come back to when I have free time, and things I'm still getting better at.

“I'd rather be genuinely interested in something than pretend to be an expert at it.”

Chapter 04 — Still Figuring Things Out

I don't have it
all figured out yet.

I'm pretty good at math, even though it isn't exactly my favourite subject. Some things come easier to me than others — and I'm still learning what I actually want to spend my time on.

I don't think someone my age needs to already know exactly who they're going to be. Right now I'm trying different things, paying attention to what feels right, and letting the picture come together slowly.

01

02
